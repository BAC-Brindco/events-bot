# Phase 0 feed verification: India Tier 2 (EOD digest) + Tier 3 (weekly digest)

Probed live on 2026-10-01 (IST, Indian IP) with plain `httpx` (desktop Chrome UA, at most 1 req/s per host). Headless Chrome (Playwright, `channel="chrome"`) was used only to check reachability where httpx was blocked.
Scripts: `docs/phase0/probes/india_t23_probe.py` (endpoint probe, writes `raw/india_t23/_summary.json` plus 50KB samples) and `docs/phase0/probes/india_t23_pib_scan.py` (PIB PRID window scan for echo and lag).
Evidence: `docs/phase0/raw/india_t23/`.

**Bottom line:** two sources in this group have a real RSS feed: TRAI and NITI Aayog. Both are site-wide Drupal `rss.xml` feeds that hold only 10 items. Nobody in the group publishes an ICS file or a documented API. Several sites run on a JS front end with an **undocumented** JSON backend that works with plain httpx: the NIC "next-gen" WordPress sites (DoT, MeitY, Power), ECI, the CDSCO NSQ table and the PNGRB JS array. These are cheaper and more stable to poll than HTML. They are still unofficial, so each needs a health check. NPCI is the only source that is hard-blocked for httpx (Akamai/F5 403). PIB is a weak echo channel for this group (see "Lag evidence").

## URL patterns

- **TRAI**: press release `https://www.trai.gov.in/notifications/press-release/<slug>`; RSS links also use `/node/<nid>` (no alias) and `/what-s-new/<slug>`. Duplicate items can appear (same title, two URLs, 7 min apart on 2026-10-01). Dedupe on title + date.
- **IRDAI**: `https://irdai.gov.in/web/guest/document-detail?documentId=<int>` (monotonic id); listing pages `/press-releases`, `/circulars`, `/notices1`, `/monthly-business-figures1`.
- **PFRDA**: `https://www.pfrda.org.in/w/<slug>` (no id or date in URL).
- **NPCI**: `https://www.npci.org.in/api/<resource>?locale=en`; PDFs at `/uploads/<Name>_<hash>.pdf`.
- **CERC**: `https://cercind.gov.in/<YYYY>/Orders/<petition>.pdf`, `/<YYYY>/whatsnew/<name>.pdf`, `/<YYYY>/draft_reg/...`; listing `recent_orders.html` (current year) and `recent_orders<YYYY>.html` (prior years).
- **NIC WP-JSON sites (DoT/MeitY/Power)**: `https://<host>/cms/wp-json/post-page/{whats_new|documents?sort=acf&limit=N&page=P|post?id=N}`; file ids in `acf_data.file[]` resolve to media posts. Same stack and same Next.js buildId on DoT and Power, so a breaking change will likely hit all three at once.
- **PNGRB**: whats-new JS array; files `../pdf/public-notice/YYYYMMDD_<x>.pdf`, `../data-bank/YYYYMMDD-CGD-MIS-Report.pdf` (date in file name).
- **PPAC**: `https://ppac.gov.in/download.php?file=<section>/<unix_epoch>_<name>.pdf`, with section in {importantnews, whatsnew, rep_studies, menu, circularnotices, govtnotlaws}.
- **CDSCO**: `https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/...`; NSQ JSON `https://cdscoonline.gov.in/CDSCO/publicNsqDrugTable`.
- **SIAM**: `https://www.siam.in/pressrelease-details.aspx?mpgid=53&pgidtrail=50&pid=<int>` (sequential, 621 newest).
- **FADA**: `https://www.fada.in/images/press-release/<14-hex><Title>.pdf` (spaces in URL).
- **PRS**: `https://prsindia.org/billtrack/<bill-slug>`.
- **ECI**: `https://www.eci.gov.in/eci-backend/public/api/{get-event|get-file|get-banner}?categories=<enc>&limit=<enc>`.
- **PIB**: `https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=<int>`. PRIDs are global across all regional offices and languages (~300+/day) and **not strictly time-ordered**.

## Lag evidence

Two releases were not captured: one ReadTimeout and about 20 PRIDs in 2316760 to 2316779. Of those, **263 were English "PIB Delhi" releases**, and those were checked against each source's own timestamps. Subset saved as `raw/india_t23/pib_scan_english_delhi_2315400-2317620.csv`.

**Takeaways**
- PIB echoes **TRAI** press releases (under Ministry of Communications) at -11 to +66 min, and **NITI Aayog** and **ECI** use PIB as their primary press channel (PIB beats NITI's own site by hours). For these three, PIB is a valid primary or secondary channel.
- PIB does **not** echo statutory regulators (IRDAI, PFRDA, CERC, PNGRB), PPAC price notifications, CDSCO NSQ data, NPCI stats or SIAM/FADA. Ministry PIB output (Power, DoT, MeitY, Health, Petroleum) is mostly ministerial or campaign news. Their notices and orders on their own sites do not appear on PIB, and some PIB items (CAFE norms) do not appear on the ministry site either. **Both channels are needed** for Power, Petroleum and DoT.
- The source sites' own posting lag is negligible where a timestamp exists: TRAI/NITI RSS, WP-JSON `post_date_gmt`, PPAC epoch, PNGRB `created_date`, ECI `date_of_creation`. CERC order-to-upload is a median of 0 days, p90 2 days (485 rows). NPCI UPI Aug-26 stats were created 1 Sep 17:04 IST. FADA's PDF has Last-Modified 7 Sep 09:00 IST, matching its "07th September'26" dateline. SIAM's August release came out 15 Sep.
- Caveat: this is a 4.5-day window. "Not echoed" means not observed in that window, which is a strong signal for the high-volume sources (CERC, PNGRB, PPAC) but weak for low-cadence ones (PFRDA, CDSCO alerts).

## No reliable feed — recommendations

Value weighting is for a SMID-cap India equity book. "Monitored scraper" means option (1): an approved, low-frequency poller with a health check that alerts when the parse yields 0 items, the newest date goes stale beyond the expected cadence, or the HTTP status or content-type changes.

## Flags

1. **PIB English RSS is redirected to Hindi** from this Indian IP: `RssMain.aspx?ModId=6&Lang=1&Regid=3` returns 302 to `Lang=2&reg=48`. Even when it works, the feed has 20 items, no `pubDate` and no ministry tag, so it cannot support "filter by ministry tag" (SPEC). This conflicts with SPEC's "PIB is the single ingestion point". Escalate to the PIB/Tier-1 owner.
2. **PIB echo is selective** (measured over 263 English PIB Delhi releases, 26 Sep to 1 Oct). TRAI (via Ministry of Communications), NITI and ECI are echoed. IRDAI, PFRDA, CERC, PNGRB, PPAC, CDSCO, NPCI, SIAM and FADA had **zero** PIB releases despite posting items in the window, and ministry notices and orders (Power, DoT) were not echoed either. "PIB as single ingestion point" cannot cover Tier 2. Disagree with that part of SPEC for Tier 2.
