SEBI RSS Feed

RSS - Rich Site Summary / Really Simple Syndication - is an application of XML (Extended Mark-up Language), that provides an open method of automatically feeding headlines, links, article summaries from website. Users can have constantly updated content from web site, delivered via a piece of software - RSS reader - specifically tailored to receive these types of feeds.

SEBI RSS Feed includes latest Press Releases, Circulars, Orders / Rulings.

To subscribe, add the URL https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebirss.xml to your RSS Reader or Click here
